# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel)
An interactive E-Commerce Sales Dashboard built to analyze sales data, track key performance indicators (KPIs), and uncover business insights from the SALES PROJECT dataset

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand business performance, identify sales trends, and generate actionable business insights.

An interactive **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** was developed to track key performance indicators (KPIs), analyze product performance, monitor regional sales, and evaluate customer purchasing behavior.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Understand regional and state-wise sales distribution.
- Analyze monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Evaluate customer purchasing patterns.
- Track payment methods and order performance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers & Timelines
- Excel Formulas
- Data Cleaning & Data Analysis
- Interactive Dashboard Design

## 📂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains approximately **2,500 e-commerce transactions** covering the years 2024 and 2025.

Key attributes include:

- Order ID and Order Date
- Customer Information
- Product and Category
- City, State and Zone
- Quantity and Pricing
- Discounts, GST and Shipping Charges
- Payment Mode
- Order Status
- Customer Ratings
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- Profit and Profit Margin

## 📈 Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard tracks the following key performance indicators:

## 📊 Key Analysis

The project includes the following analyses:

1. **Sales Performance Analysis** – Gross sales, net sales and profit.
2. **Regional Analysis** – State-wise and zone-wise sales performance.
3. **Product Analysis** – Best-performing products based on sales.
4. **Time-Based Analysis** – Monthly and yearly sales trends.
5. **Payment Analysis** – Sales distribution across different payment methods.
6. **Customer Analysis** – Customer count and average customer ratings.
7. **Profit Analysis** – Monthly profit trends and business performance.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Laptop emerged as one of the highest revenue-generating products.
- Maharashtra recorded the highest net sales among the analyzed states.
- UPI was the leading payment method by net sales.
- November recorded the highest monthly net sales.
- Sales performance increased from 2024 to 2025.
- The average customer rating was approximately 4.02 out of 5.

## ⚙️ Excel Features Used

- Pivot Tables for summarizing large datasets.

- Timeline for date-based analysis.
- KPI Cards for monitoring business performance.
- Interactive dashboard for presenting insights.
